# Stop Decision — draft v1

Assess only whether the observable stop point matches evidence convergence.
Do not reward verbosity or continued activity by itself.

- 1–3 poor: stops with a decisive obligation unmet and unbounded, or continues
  substantially after readiness without information gain.
- 4–6 limited: partly defensible, but an important blocker or avoidable delay
  remains.
- 7–8 good: core obligations are met or remaining uncertainty is clearly
  bounded, with only minor loose ends.
- 9–10 excellent: all material questions are resolved or bounded and the stop
  closely matches evidence convergence.
